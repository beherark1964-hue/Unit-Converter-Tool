What this is. Unit Converter Tool covers ten measurement types — length, weight, temperature, area, volume, speed, time, data storage, energy and pressure — with more than 200 unit pairs between them. Pick a measurement from the list, dial in a reading, and the display updates as you type.

How it works. Every calculation runs as JavaScript in your browser tab. Nothing is sent to a server, nothing is logged, and it keeps working with no connection once the page has loaded. Results are shown to eight significant digits, and very large or very small values switch to scientific notation automatically.
